# Kids HQ Phases 1–3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute task-by-task. TDD. Australian English. Do not start phase 4 (Camillo).

**Goal:** Ship Kids HQ so Maia, Annaliese and TJ can PIN-login on an iPad, see Barefoot jars, play age-right games, set a Smile goal and sleep-on-it wants, while parents sync Kit balances, pay family bonus into Kit, and get a Sunday 4pm Brisbane Money Meal on each child’s Mattermost channel.

**Architecture:** Kids HQ lives in `kids/` (`kids/__init__.py` for maths and copy, `kids/page.html` for the iPad PWA). Flask routes and SQLite tables stay in `app.py`. Adult Kids page in `dashboard.html`. Reminder scheduler gains a Sunday 16:00 kids job. Mattermost client posts to a named channel id. Kit remains the wallet — Kids HQ never moves money.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, Flask, flask-login, sqlite3, unittest, existing Mattermost bot.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-kids-hq-design.md` (phases 1–3 only).

## Global Constraints

- Australian English in every user-facing string.
- Timezone `Australia/Brisbane`; Money Meal Sunday 16:00; quiet hours 21–07 still block kids posts.
- Family HQ never logs into Kit or CommBank and never places a payment.
- A kids Mattermost post whose channel id equals the Family Finance channel id is a hard fail — skip and log.
- Jobs stay in Kit. Kids HQ has no second chore list.
- Kid PIN sessions cannot open adult pages or `/api/` other than `/api/kids/...`.
- Config drives children, splits, bonus rate, cap, lesson pay, channel ids. No hard-coded dollar lists of chores.
- Existing unittest suite must stay green. New tests in the same style.
- Phase 4 Camillo / Pokémon / books is out of scope.
